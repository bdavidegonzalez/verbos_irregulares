Conectores en Inglés (Linking Words)

1. Conectores de Causa y Razón

ConectorSignificadoEjemplo
becauseporqueI stayed home because it was raining.
sinceya que, puesto queSince you’re here, let’s start the meeting.
ascomo, dado queAs it was late, we decided to leave.
forporque (formal)He must be hungry, for he hasn’t eaten all day.

2. Conectores de Consecuencia

ConectorSignificadoEjemplo
soasí queIt was raining, so we stayed inside.
thereforepor lo tantoHe was ill, therefore he couldn’t come.
thusasí, de este modo (formal)She worked hard, thus she succeeded.
hencede ahí, por eso (formal)He didn’t study, hence his bad grades.

3. Conectores de Contraste y Oposición

ConectorSignificadoEjemplo
butperoI like coffee, but I prefer tea.
howeversin embargoIt was cold. However, we went swimming.
although / thoughaunqueAlthough it was late, they kept working.
even thoughaunque (enfatizado)Even though he was tired, he continued.
while / whereasmientras queShe likes jazz, while I prefer rock.
yetsin embargoIt’s simple, yet effective.

4. Conectores de Adición

ConectorSignificadoEjemplo
andyShe bought apples and oranges.
alsotambiénHe is smart and also kind.
tootambién (al final)I like pizza too.
as welltambiénShe plays the piano as well.
in additionademásIn addition, we need more volunteers.
moreover / furthermoreademás (formal)The plan is simple. Moreover, it’s cheap.

5. Conectores de Tiempo y Secuencia

ConectorSignificadoEjemplo
whencuandoI called you when I arrived.
whilemientrasShe was cooking while I was cleaning.
beforeantes de queWash your hands before you eat.
afterdespués de queI’ll call you after the meeting.
thenluegoFirst we went shopping, then we had lunch.
nexta continuaciónNext, you should press the button.
finallyfinalmenteFinally, we reached the hotel.

6. Conectores de Condición

ConectorSignificadoEjemplo
ifsiIf it rains, we’ll stay home.
unlessa menos queYou can’t go out unless you finish homework.
as long assiempre queYou can borrow it as long as you return it.
provided thatcon tal de queYou can come, provided that you behave.

7. Conectores de Comparación

ConectorSignificadoEjemplo
likecomoHe runs like a professional.
as…astan…comoShe is as tall as her brother.
thanque (comparativo)This book is better than that one.
