🧳 The Journey Begins

Yesterday, I left my house early in the morning. I had a backpack, a bottle of water, and a map. I chose to go alone, but I felt excited.

I began my trip walking through the forest. Suddenly, I found a little dog. It came to me and gave me a stick. Maybe it wanted to play!

I drove to the nearest town, and there I bought some food. Then I ate a sandwich and drank some juice. Delicious!

Later, I heard music, so I went to the park. A group of people were dancing. I didn’t know them, but they made me feel welcome.

At the end of the day, I got home tired, but happy. It had been a great adventure!
