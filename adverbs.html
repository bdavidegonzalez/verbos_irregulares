Adverbios de Tiempo y Frecuencia

1. Simple Time Adverbs

Adverbio Uso / Significado Ejemplo
NowAhoraI am writing this sentence right now.
YesterdayAyerI went to the park yesterday.
TodayHoyThere’s a lot of sun today!
TomorrowMañanaWe are leaving for our vacation tomorrow.
SoonProntoThe movie will start soon.
LaterMás tardeWe’ll eat later.
EarlyTempranoShe always wakes up early.
LateTardeI apologize for being late.
BrieflyBrevementeShe talked briefly and then stopped.
MomentarilyMomentáneamenteThe sun disappeared momentarily, but then it came back.
ForeverPara siempreI will love you forever.
TemporarilyTemporalmenteHe is living with his friend temporarily.

2. Time Adverbs with Prepositions

Estructura / Adverbio Uso / Significado Ejemplo
BeforeAntesPlease finish your homework before going to bed.
AfterDespuésWe go to the movies after finishing our work.
DuringDuranteI read a book during my lunch break.
SinceDesdeI have been studying Spanish since last year.
UntilHastaYou can stay up until 10 p.m.
WhileMientrasHe was watching TV while eating dinner.
In (the morning)En (la mañana)I have to go to the doctor in the morning.
On (Monday)En (el lunes)I’ll be in London on Friday.
At (6 o’clock)A (las 6 en punto)I’ll see you at 6 o’clock.
From…to…Desde… hasta…The sports class lasts from 9 to 10 am.
ForDurante / PorShe’s been waiting for him for 2 hours.

3. Frequency Adverbs

Adverbio Uso / Significado Posición Ejemplo
AlwaysSiempreAntes del verbo (o después de "to be")He hasn’t always agreed with me.
UsuallyUsualmenteAntes del verboThey usually eat out on the weekend.
OftenA menudoAntes del verboWe don’t often go to bed late.
SometimesA vecesAntes del verbo / al inicio o finalDo you sometimes travel abroad?
NeverNuncaAntes del verbo / después de "to be"I never go to the barber.
DailyA diarioGeneralmente al finalShe exercises daily.
WeeklySemanalmenteGeneralmente al finalWe meet weekly.
MonthlyMensualmenteGeneralmente al finalThey pay the rent monthly.
YearlyAnualmenteGeneralmente al finalThe event is held yearly.

📌 Reglas clave de posición:

  • Con verbos principales: el adverbio va antes del verbo → I never go to the barber.
  • Con verbo “to be”: el adverbio va después del verbo → I am never late for class.
  • Con adverbios de frecuencia específica (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly): suelen ir al final → She exercises daily.
